02
Merchant growth strategy

Acquire merchants who are ready to sell.

The goal is to maximize the quality and economic potential of the merchant base, not simply the number of leads. Different acquisition channels serve different segments and scale at different marginal costs.

Who we target

Modeled first-sale mix vs. initial targeting effort · 16 months

81% of projected first-sale merchants come from established social sellers and small omnichannel retailers. Prioritize existing demand and fulfillment readiness while offering education-first pathways to earlier-stage merchants.

How we acquire

Direct CAC excludes activation program, payroll and tools. The lower-cost channels are not infinitely scalable.

Portfolio strategy: optimize quality, not just cost

Calculation method and baseline reconciliation

First-sale GMV × activation factor × AOV factor + repeat GMV × activation × retained activity × repeat orders × AOV + incremental program GMV × program response × AOV. Integrated GMV = total GMV × min(100%, 35% × adoption factor). Gross fees use the workbook’s aggregate gross-fees-to-integrated-GMV ratio. Contribution = gross fees × (recognition share − variable cost share). M16 activity follows the workbook scenario formula for new, repeat and incremental merchants. Custom activity is capped only to prevent impossible values; the three workbook presets are preserved exactly.

Separate cost stress test

Cost inflation is not assumed to improve GMV or treatment response. Move the two factors independently.

Capital release rule: stop or redesign channels that fail quality or capacity checks. Require controlled lifecycle lift and validated vendor cost before expansion. No future CLARA, churn or propensity uplift is credited in the base forecast.

Data foundation and deployment

Merchant identityOrder lifecycleGMV / fee ledgerChannel attributionProduct eventsIntervention exposureSupport interactions

Foundation: governed event definitions, deduplication, completed order timestamps, exposure and holdout logs, service adoption and contribution data. Confirm consent, access control and data retention.

Delivery: begin with reproducible rules and cohort reports; train only after label maturity and adequate sample size; score in scheduled batches; deliver prioritized queues via CRM; capture outcomes and monitor drift.

Operating discipline: use experiments to measure causal impact. Model performance alone is insufficient. Product and merchant success teams own execution; BI owns validation and monitoring.

Proposed sequencing only, conditional on data and engineering readiness, shared capacity and management approval. Additional investment is not automatically funded from the remaining cash.

08 / The way forward

The objective is not simply a bigger merchant base.
It is a smarter, more active and more valuable merchant economy.

Acquire intelligently. Activate effectively. Retain proactively. Monetize sustainably. Learn continuously.

1 / Approve the learning agenda

Validate merchant qualification, first-sale conversion, repeat behavior and fee economics before treating assumptions as facts.

2 / Authorize gated execution

Confirm the funding ceilings, shared-team capacity, pilot delivery and the ₱1M protected contingency; release scale capital only against evidence.

3 / Establish decision science

Align on data access, operating owners and a roadmap from segmentation to predictive and prescriptive decisions.

The proposed growth strategy is not a guarantee of payback. It is a disciplined investment hypothesis and a framework for learning which merchant decisions create economic value.
