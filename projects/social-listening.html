Business challenge

Understand the customer experience beyond formal feedback

Customers often talk more openly in public communities than they do in structured surveys or support tickets. Those conversations can reveal uncertainty around repayments, frustration with delays, service failures, and how the brand is being compared with other digital banks.

Identify recurring pain points

Group unstructured conversation into interpretable themes across lending, payments, collections, app issues, and broader digital banking comparisons.

Separate sentiment from emotion

Understand whether a negative tone reflects confusion, frustration, fear, or another emotional state—and why that matters for customer experience.

Track how the narrative evolves

Compare annual conversations to see when general exploration shifts into specific operational friction and repayment-related concern.

NLP methodology

From public conversation to structured signals

1Collect and organize text

I combined public Reddit posts, titles, and comments into a common text dataset and preserved content type so I could compare how people expressed concerns in different parts of a thread.

2Clean multilingual text

I normalized noisy text, handled English and Tagalog stopwords, and prepared code-switched content for tokenization and downstream NLP analysis.

3Score sentiment and emotion

I used nlptown/bert-base-multilingual-uncased-sentiment for five-level sentiment classification and joeddav/xlm-roberta-large-xnli for zero-shot emotion labeling.

4Discover themes

I explored BERTopic, LDA, n-grams, and aspect-style groupings to identify business-readable themes from public discussion.

5Interpret the customer story

I compared topics, emotions, and sentiment across 2024, 2025, and 2026 to understand how the conversation evolved.

Why multiple lenses matter: sentiment tells me whether the tone is positive or negative, but topic modeling and emotion analysis help explain what customers are actually reacting to.

My reading of the topic structure

Repayment clarity: customers were asking where to pay, whether payments were reflected, and how collections communication related to their obligations.

Operational trust: app issues and unresolved support requests had an outsized impact because they occurred during moments tied to access to money.

Competitive context: comparisons still mattered, but by 2026 they were no longer the dominant theme of the conversation.

Business interpretation

Three priorities surfaced from the analysis

01 · Make repayment clearer

Customers need clearer repayment instructions, visible payment confirmation, and an easier way to verify loan status or balance updates.

02 · Improve collections communication

Collections-related conversations pointed to a need for more consistent, understandable, and customer-oriented repayment communication.

03 · Strengthen reliability and support

App access issues, failed transactions, and unresolved service requests undermine trust at moments when customers expect reliability the most.

Why this work matters

Social listening is valuable because it adds context that structured operational metrics often miss. By connecting topic modeling, sentiment, emotion, and time-based comparisons, I was able to turn unstructured customer voice into a more actionable view of friction across Product, Operations, Collections, and Customer Experience.
