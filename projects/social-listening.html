Social Listening & Sentiment Intelligence

I built a multilingual NLP analysis workflow to understand how customers discuss digital banking online. By combining sentiment classification, emotion detection and topic modeling, I traced how the conversation evolved between 2024 and 2026—and identified the experiences shaping customer trust.

Business challenge

Understand the customer experience beyond formal feedback

Customers routinely share financial experiences in public communities—often when they are comparing banks, waiting for disbursements, troubleshooting transfers or trying to understand repayment obligations. These conversations offer a different view of customer experience from structured operational KPIs.

Identify recurring pain points

Group thousands of unstructured words into interpretable themes across digital banking, payments and lending.

Separate sentiment from emotion

Understand not only whether discussion is negative, but whether it reflects frustration, fear, surprise or trust.

See how perceptions evolve

Compare annual conversations to uncover emerging friction and where customer expectations are most likely to break down.

NLP methodology

From public conversations to structured signals

1Data preparation

Combined public Reddit posts, post titles and comments into a common text dataset, retaining content type to compare how different parts of a discussion express sentiment.

2Multilingual text cleaning

Normalized text, filtered noise and developed English and Tagalog stopword handling to improve token-based analysis of code-switched conversations.

3Transformer-based sentiment and emotion

Applied nlptown/bert-base-multilingual-uncased-sentiment for five-level sentiment scoring and joeddav/xlm-roberta-large-xnli for zero-shot emotion classification against a defined set of emotion labels.

4Topic and aspect discovery

Explored Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA), BERTopic, n-grams, frequent terms and rule-based aspect tags. Reviewed model-generated topics and converted them into business-readable issue categories.

5Segment and communicate

Compared themes, sentiment and emotions across years and text types, then translated the results into practical customer-experience priorities.

Analytical design: I used separate topic, sentiment and emotion lenses because a negative star score alone cannot reveal whether a customer is confused about repayment, frustrated by an unresolved support ticket or simply comparing products.
Exploratory data analysis

Three distinct stages of customer conversation

2024Exploration and bank comparisons

General digital bank comparisons accounted for 81.1% of the 2024 topic-classification sample.

2025Growing operational friction

Disbursement and funding: 16.8%. Loan repayment and collections: 16.6%.

2026Repayment concerns become dominant

Loan repayment and collections represented 57.1% of the 2026 classified-topic sample.

What changed across the years

2024

People were largely exploring digital banks, comparing rates, sharing suggestions and discussing alternatives. The discussion was more about evaluation than firsthand negative experience.

2025

Conversations broadened into delays in loan disbursement, uncertainty around repayment, savings concerns and support experiences. Customer questions became more operational and personal.

2026

Repayment and collections dominated the analyzed topics. Discussion centered on payment clarity, third-party collection contact, disputed balances and difficulty resolving service issues.

Annual datasets contain different numbers of public posts, titles and comments. These distributions describe the collected conversation samples, not the bank's entire customer population.

What the topics revealed

Repayment clarity: Some customers were uncertain where to pay, whether payments had posted, or how restructuring and third-party collections affected their obligations.

System reliability: Failed transactions, login problems and unresolved tickets created frustration during moments involving access to money.

Competitive discovery: Customers continued comparing digital bank features and rates, but this was a smaller part of the 2026 discussion than service-related issues.

Business interpretation

Three priorities surfaced from the analysis

01 · Make repayment clear

Standardize payment instructions, confirm successful payments and make loan status easy to verify. Ambiguity around balances and collection agents can undermine trust even when a customer wants to repay.

02 · Improve the collections experience

Focus on consistent, helpful communication during high-stress repayment situations. Customers need understandable next steps, not competing or confusing messages.

03 · Strengthen reliability and support

Prioritize transaction errors, account access and unresolved support requests. These failures affect customers at the moments they most need confidence in their bank.

Why this work matters

The value of social listening is not a sentiment score by itself. By connecting topics, emotions and changes over time, I was able to translate unstructured conversations into a clearer view of customer friction and specific areas for investigation by Product, Operations, Collections and Customer Experience teams.

I report the analyzed sample and model outputs as research findings. The project does not claim that these recommendations were implemented or that they produced measured improvements.
